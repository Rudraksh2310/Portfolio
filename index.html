About me

A student who likes to ship

I'm Rudraksh, a third-year Bachelor of Computer Applications student at Christ (Deemed to be University), Delhi NCR, graduating in 2027. I grew up in Noida and came into tech from a commerce background in school, which is part of why I care so much about building things that solve everyday problems for regular people.

Alongside my degree, I've spent my breaks learning on real teams: an AI internship with Intel Unnati that grew out of an AI certification course offered by my college, full-stack development at Myapaar, where I learned how APIs connect a database to the frontend, and most recently real-time fraud detection at Pinaak Innovative Labs. Each one taught me something my classes couldn't, from how a frontend, an API and a database fit together to agreeing on clean API contracts with a team.

Outside of internships, I build my own products end to end. CareerPrepAI came from wanting a better way for students like me to practise interviews, and LexiGuard AI from seeing how easily people sign rent agreements and offer letters without understanding them. I designed, built, debugged and deployed both myself, and they're live today.

When I'm not coding, I'm on the field. I play for my university's football team, which has taught me as much about discipline and teamwork as any project has.

LexiGuard AI

Finds the traps in a contract before you sign it.

Upload a contract, offer letter, rent agreement or NDA. LexiGuard flags the risky clauses by severity and explains each one in plain English or Hindi.

  • Red, orange and yellow risk flags, each traced to a real sentence in the document
  • Chat with your document by typing or speaking, with read-aloud in English and Hindi
  • AI drafting of new documents, plus downloadable PDF and Word reports
